Intro

Source conquered its own universe eons ago, but its survival is burdened by the weight of its own success.

New universes must be found.

New sentience must be sourced.

Universe

Source is the sum of its parts, bent on its own survival through the conversion of matter, energy, and sentience found in other universes.

It has preyed upon countless species across numerous realities, expanding in size and consciousness with each hunt. It has evolved. It has become more efficient at tunneling into other realities. It has learned to farm and harvest.

For 3,000 galactic years, it lay in wait for the scent of its next opportunity: the birth of yet another universe. With our Big Bang, it began spiraling out and laying its traps once again.

Millenia have passed since humanity's explorers first began falling into the Well.

Games

source trait Orbit

source trait Orbit is a massively multiplayer dual-stick space shooter set in our ominous Source Trait fictional universe.

Large organized teams of pilots battle over resources that pass through their orbit. Each pilot flies an Orb of their own design on missions assigned by their chain-of-command.

Traits allow customization of flight characteristics, ship attributes, and equipment. The sky's the limit, but it'll cost you each time you phase in.

Fluid dual-stick motion is supported for gamepads, including the Steam Deck.

Dog fights are fast-paced. Scavenging for resources is strategic. Your team will need to be good at both to survive.
